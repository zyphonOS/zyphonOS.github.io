What we will not do

The refusals are part of the offer.

  • Quote a price before scope is agreed.
  • Promise a delivery date we have not costed.
  • Publish a client’s work without written permission.
  • Claim a capability we cannot show, or a metric we cannot read.
  • Manufacture urgency, invent stock, or write a testimonial for somebody else.

If one of those refusals costs us a deal, it stays refused. A deal that needs a false number to close is a deal that ends in a dispute.

The community · not open yet

An empty room, labelled honestly.

There is no community here yet. Not a small one, not a waitlist of ten thousand, none. This section exists so that the day it opens it opens where it was always going to be, rather than being announced as though it had been there all along.

What it will be: the people the members actually serve. Businesses that buy documentation, households that buy power, listeners who follow the artist, builders who want the agent wallet. Not a forum for its own sake.

What it will not be: a number on this page pretending to be a movement.

When it opens

When there is something to join for, and at least one person has already received it. Until then this stays a label, not a link.
