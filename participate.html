Ways in

Seven doors. Pick the right one.

Each route asks for what is needed to answer you properly, and nothing more. None of them asks you to give up ownership of anything.

What participation never costs

You keep what is yours.

  • Your business, brand, catalogue and client relationships stay yours.
  • No default equity, no forced consolidation, no silent control.
  • Anything exchanged commercially is scoped, priced and written down.
  • You can leave. No permission is required.

Honest expectations

What happens after you write.

  • 01Your message is read by a person, not a sequence.
  • 02If there is a fit, you get a specific reply with a specific next step.
  • 03If there is not, you are told that too, rather than left waiting.
  • 04Nothing is added to a mailing list, sold, or circulated.

This ecosystem is early. It is not a large community, and it is not presented as one. That is precisely why an individual message gets attention.
