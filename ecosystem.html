Refused

What this model refuses to draw.

  • A parent company above the four. There is none.
  • A line between EBVKA and WillWired. There is no agreement between them, so none is drawn.
  • Nested boxes. Nesting is ownership language, and this model has no nesting.
  • An independence edge. Independence is a property of a participant and cannot be a connection.

Boundary

What participation does not mean.

Being part of this ecosystem does not give one participant a claim on another’s revenue, its name, its audience or its decisions. It does not transfer equity, and it does not make one participant the parent of another.

Where money moves between participants, it moves under written terms with a named counterparty, a scope, a price and an acceptance condition. Where those do not exist yet, this site says draft or none.

Equity, where it is ever discussed, is discussed after a participant has reached its first revenue milestone. Until then it is not a topic, and it is not a hidden one.

The three words

Manages

By agreement, revocable, scoped. Never ownership.

Aligned

Shared intent, one front door. No equity implied.

Independent

Self-owned. Keeps its own name and its own money.
