What this diagram refuses to draw

  • A parent company above the four. There is none.
  • A line between EBVKA and WillWired. There is no agreement between them, so none is drawn.
  • Nested boxes. Nesting is ownership language and the model has no nesting.
  • An independence edge. Independence is a property of the card, and cannot be a connection.

What participation does not mean

Being part of this ecosystem does not transfer ownership, equity or creative control. Pikkaio may develop and manage a participant under an agreed relationship; that relationship is management, not ownership. ZyphonOS provides intelligence and infrastructure without defining who a participant is.

Where an agreement changes on paper, this register changes the same day. A stale register would be a false claim.
