The stage

Stated plainly.

ZyphonOS has not yet earned revenue from a customer. We say so on a public page because a stage that is hidden becomes a claim that is false, and because the first paying customer is the only milestone that counts as progress.

When a client project can be shown, it will carry the client’s own words and a date — not a testimonial written by us.

What counts as progress here

Money received and logged, an object somebody can inspect, and a decision-critical fact learned. Not activity, not a build month, and not a plan.
