ZyphonOS

Technology and research

  • Agent and execution infrastructurein development

    A local agent runtime that holds intent, executes work, and records what it actually did. Built for ordinary operational work rather than demonstration.

  • Verification and honesty gatesactive

    Deterministic checks that refuse to pass when evidence is missing: a claim is not a completion, an unknown is not a zero, a stale fact is not a current one.

  • Continuity and documentation systemsactive

    Structured operating knowledge so work survives interruptions, machine failures and changes of session — the least glamorous and most load-bearing part of the whole system.

  • The AOSI research questionresearch

    Whether an organically integrated intelligence is possible, and what preserving intent and honest continuity would require. An open question, not a delivered system.

A note on proof

Nothing here is dressed up.

There are no client logos on this page, no testimonials, and no numbers offered as results. That is deliberate: a public site that manufactures credibility is the fastest way to lose it with exactly the people worth talking to.

Where work has been done, it is described. Where it is only intended, it is labelled. If you want to see something specific before you engage, ask — and you will be told plainly whether it exists.
