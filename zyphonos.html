Technology that reports on itself.

ZyphonOS builds intelligent systems — agents, software and infrastructure — and states which parts work, which do not, and which are only being attempted.

Four labels, applied without exception

Implemented

What exists today

An agent kernel that runs on an ordinary machine, a verification and accountability ledger where a completion claim is not counted until a row from outside the claimer names the channel and the value actually read, and this site.

That is a short list. It is the true one. Capabilities are added to it when they can be inspected, not when they can be described.

Research · not a claim

AOSI

ZyphonOS is researching what it calls AOSI: an artificial organic superintelligence. That is the ambition, stated as an ambition. There is no finished product behind the name, no demonstration of consciousness, and no claim that software is alive.

In any public artefact the name AOSI appears as what is being tested, never as what has been proven.

Why the room is dark

This is the only dark room in the ecosystem. It is where machines are read: values, sources, timestamps and failures. The rest of the site is paper, because the people are the point and the machines are the instrument.

If a reading cannot be read, it is shown as unknown with the reason. It is never shown as zero, and no value is ever animated.
