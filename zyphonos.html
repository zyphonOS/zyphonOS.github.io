01 — Technology & research

ZyphonOS

Agents, software and execution infrastructure — and a research question that has not been answered yet.

What it does

Turning stated intent into work that can be checked.

Most software asks a person to translate their intent into a tool's language. ZyphonOS works the other way: hold the intent, do the work, and prove what was actually done.

That means building systems that keep a record of what they claimed and what a third party could verify — because the failure mode of AI systems is not usually being wrong, it is being confidently wrong with no way to notice.

The work is deliberately unglamorous: infrastructure, tooling, process, documentation, and checks that refuse to pass when the evidence is missing.

Where things actually stand

Four labels, used strictly.

Anything that cannot be shown here with evidence does not belong here.

  • Agent and execution infrastructureimplemented, in development

    Local agent runtime, tooling, task and state handling, and a working loop that can be inspected and corrected. Actively developed, not a finished product.

  • Verification and honesty gatesimplemented

    Deterministic checks that refuse to pass without evidence: claims must be verified against a live channel, unknowns stay unknown, and disputes are recorded rather than smoothed over.

  • Documentation and knowledge systemsimplemented

    Structured operating knowledge, decision records and continuity practice — built so work survives an interruption, a machine, or a person.

  • Intelligence research — the AOSI questionresearch

    How far intent can be preserved; how a system maintains honest continuity of self; how capability can be evaluated without flattering itself. Stated as research, never as achievement.

The boundary

What this is not.

  • Not a claim of sentience, subjective experience, permanent awareness or achieved superintelligence.
  • Not an autonomous company running itself. Decisions that matter stay with people.
  • Not a promise of general capability. Each system is built for a problem, and says so.
  • Not finished. The interesting part is still being worked on.

Who it is for

People building something real.

AI builders and developers who care whether a system can be trusted. Researchers who want a working test bed rather than a slide. Businesses with a repeated problem that deserves a proper build rather than another licence. Partners with capability that compounds.

If you want a conversation about a project, a technology partnership, or the research direction, use the enquiry route below. Be specific about the problem; that is the useful part.
