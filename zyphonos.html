Research · not a claim

AOSI. ZyphonOS is researching what it calls an artificial organic superintelligence: a system that grows, holds continuity and reports on itself. That is the ambition, stated as an ambition. There is no finished product behind the name, no demonstration of consciousness, and no claim that software is alive.

Why the readings look like this

A reading is a channel, not an opinion.

This room shows values the way an instrument would: with the channel they came from and the moment they were read.

  • A missing reading is unknown, with a reason. It is never rendered as zero, because zero is a measurement and absence is not.
  • No value is animated or counted up. A number that moves on its own is decoration, and decoration is where a stale figure hides.
  • A failed check is published. If a surface did not answer, that is the reading.
  • Claims are counted last. The ledger counts what was verified, not what was announced.
