01 · The programme

What AOSI means here.

AOSI stands for artificial organic superintelligence. It is the research ambition of this ecosystem: intelligence that grows and holds continuity rather than resetting with every conversation.

It is stated here as an ambition, because that is what it is. There is no finished product behind the name, no demonstration of consciousness, and no claim that software is alive. In every public artefact on this site the name appears as what is being tested or aimed at, never as what has been proven.

The search and the boundary →

The honesty rule this site is built on

A claim is not done because someone inside the work says it is done. It is done when a record from outside the claimer names the channel and the value that was actually read.

That rule is why pages here carry status labels and read dates, and why some of them say “unknown” instead of a number.

03 · How to read this site

Four labels. Three words. Two rules.

Implemented

It exists and can be inspected. It runs.

In development

Parts exist; it is not finished, and it is not offered as if it were.

Research

We are investigating it. No claim of capability is made.

Proposed

It is intended. Nothing has been built.

Manages

By agreement, revocable, scoped. Never ownership.

Aligned

Shared intent, one front door. No equity implied.

Independent

Self-owned. Keeps its own name and its own money.

Zero and unknown are different things

A missing reading is shown as unknown, with the reason it is missing. It is never shown as zero, and no value is ever invented to fill a space.

The words of a person are theirs

Structure, evidence and cuts are prepared here. Sentences are not put into anyone’s mouth, and a participant’s voice is not rewritten to sound like this site.

One front door

Start with a conversation, not a price list.

A price without a scope is a guess, so none is published. Write to the participant you need and the scope comes first.
