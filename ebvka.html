EBVKA · independent artist and creative company

An independent artist and a company.

Music, expression, storytelling, its own catalogue, its own audience and its own intellectual property. EBVKA participates in this ecosystem voluntarily and keeps its independence.

Three registers, held distinctly

Person
The founder: a person with a life, a name and a practice, not a brand asset.
Artist
The creative identity: music, writing, performance and the work itself.
Company
The commercial identity: catalogue, rights, audience, agreements and revenue.

These are three registers of one participant, not three brands. They are kept separate because they answer different questions: who is speaking, what was made, and what was agreed.

Creative-work states

Used for creative work only. These are not company statuses and they are not maturity labels.

Performed
The work has been played or shown to an audience.
Recorded
The work exists as a recording.
Releasing
The work is being prepared for release, with a confirmed path.
Archived
The work is held and not currently being released.

Independence and management

Pikkaio develops and manages EBVKA under an agreed relationship. This does not constitute ownership. ZyphonOS provides intelligence and infrastructure without defining or absorbing EBVKA’s identity.

EBVKA’s visual identity is not yet approved, and it will not be invented here. It requires its own process and the artist’s own approval. Nothing on this site derives EBVKA’s identity from another participant’s colours.

Releases are announced when they are real. No date, distributor or streaming link is published in advance of confirmation.
