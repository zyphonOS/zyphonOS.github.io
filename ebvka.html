The story

A voice from Lagos. Not waiting to be chosen.

EBVKA is an independent artist and creative identity — a person who sings, writes and performs, and who intends to build a body of work on his own terms.

The name is self-given. That is the point. The usual route asks an artist to be selected before being heard: a label, a playlist, a gatekeeper, a yes from someone else. This route is the other one — put the work out, build the audience directly, and keep ownership of the catalogue while doing it.

The music carries the ambition of the wider ecosystem without being owned by it. The story is the same one told everywhere here: build it yourself, and name yourself. But it is told in the only way that makes an artist worth listening to — in his own voice, about his own life.
