Pikkaio · brand development and business management

Structure for the craft.

Pikkaio develops brands and manages businesses for organisations that need structure before they scale.

Scope

What Pikkaio does.

  • Brand development. Identity, positioning, naming, and the documents that carry them.
  • Business structure. What is being sold, to whom, at what price, with what terms.
  • Campaigns and commercial development. The work of reaching the people who buy.
  • Operations and execution. Documentation, process, delivery.
  • Management of participants under an agreed relationship.

Engagement

How work starts.

With a conversation, then a written scope, then an agreement. No engagement begins without written terms, and no price appears on this site because a price without a scope is a guess.

Pikkaio earns a fee on work it delivers. It does not earn from a participant’s equity by default, and managing a participant is not owning one.

Relationships

Two managements, one alignment, no parent.

Pikkaio develops and manages EBVKA under an agreed relationship. That does not constitute ownership. Pikkaio also manages WillWired under an agreed relationship, and WillWired remains Muktad’s business.

Pikkaio is an independent participant. It is not the parent of anything, and this ecosystem is not a group with a holding company. Agreements between participants are listed, with their status, in the relationship register.

Brand isolation

This room uses the site’s neutral interface. Pikkaio’s own colour and type system is applied to its own surfaces, not inherited by, or projected onto, the other participants. A brand shown in another brand’s colours is not a brand.
