WillWired · technology and power

Power that stays on.

WillWired exists to bridge the gap between ambition and technology in Africa, particularly for young professionals and technology enthusiasts.

What the business is for

Work in Lagos is interrupted by power. Hours are lost to darkness, generator fuel and dead batteries, and those hours are the business. WillWired sells technology and power that keeps the work running: products, installation and service.

The tone is plain. No fake scarcity, no invented stock, no countdown on a product nobody has confirmed.

EL NIÑO

EL NIÑO is the power campaign: reliable power presented as a practical answer rather than a luxury. Prices, stock and delivery windows are confirmed per enquiry, because an unconfirmed figure on a page becomes a promise someone relies on.

No price, stock level, warranty or delivery claim is published here. Those are confirmed in writing, per order.

Status, stated plainly
