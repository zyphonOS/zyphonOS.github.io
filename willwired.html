WillWired · power and technology

Power that stays on.

WillWired supplies and installs the power and technology that keeps work running in Lagos: portable power, solar, inverters, and the consoles people actually buy. Sizing and installation are part of the sale, not an afterthought.

What we supply

Four things, supplied and installed.

Every item is quoted for your situation. Sizing comes first, because the wrong capacity is more expensive than the right one bought later.

Portable power stations

For the work that has to continue through an outage: laptops, routers, lights, phones, screens. Sized to the hours you actually lose.

Solar and solar-ready systems

Panels, charge control and the storage behind them, for a site that should stop paying for fuel and noise every day.

Inverter systems and installation

Backup that carries a building rather than a desk: sizing, wiring, changeover and the handover walk-through that means you can run it yourself.

Consoles and accessories

Game consoles and the accessories that go with them, sourced to order. Access to technology is the business; WillWired is not a manufacturer.

Availability, price, warranty and delivery window are confirmed in writing for your order, with the date they were confirmed. Nothing on this page is a stock or price claim, and no figure here should be relied on before it is written to you.

How it works

Three steps. The first one is free.

1 · Tell us what must stay on

Five questions about your load and your hours. That is the whole ask, and it takes about two minutes.

2 · We size it and put it in writing

A recommendation with the capacity it needs, the price for your order, and the date that price was confirmed.

3 · We install, then stay reachable

Delivery, installation and a walk-through. After that you keep the same address for service, so nobody has to start the story again.
