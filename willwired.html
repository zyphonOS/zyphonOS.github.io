03 — Technology & power

WILLWIRED

Bridging the gap between ambition and technology in Africa — for young professionals and technology enthusiasts who need the tool, not the lecture.

The mission

Ambition is not the shortage. Access is.

There is no shortage of young Africans who intend to build something serious. What is missing is the ordinary infrastructure of ambition: a capable machine, a stable connection, and power that does not fail in the middle of the work.

WILLWIRED exists to close that gap in practical terms — sourcing technology people actually need at a price they can reach, and solving the power problem that quietly limits everything else.

The larger intent is not retail. It is that a person with ambition is not stopped by the absence of equipment. That is why the same business runs a commercial line and, separately, an access initiative for young people.

Three routes

Technology and equipment

active

Sourcing and supply of the technology a person or small business needs — computers and equipment for work, study and production. Recommendations are given against your actual budget and use, and where installation or setup is required it is handled with vetted partners.

EL NIÑO — reliable power

in development

An initiative built around one of the most expensive problems in Nigeria: power that is not stable. Solar and energy solutions sized to what a household or small business actually consumes, rather than a generic package sold by capacity.

Every EL NIÑO enquiry starts with a load assessment — what you need to run, for how long, and what it costs you today when the supply fails.

TAD — technology access for young people

proposed

An intended initiative to make real technology available to children and teenagers so that ambition in technology can form early, rather than being discovered late or never. Starting point: Lagos, with a wider Nigerian and African intention.

Status, stated plainly: TAD is a proposed initiative. It is not yet a legally established or operating foundation, and no programmes, beneficiaries, locations or outcomes are claimed here. Anyone interested in helping build it is welcome to make contact.

Standing

An independent business.

  • WILLWIRED is its own business with its own founder, direction and commercial relationships.
  • It participates in this ecosystem. It is not owned by it.
  • Supply, installation and delivery terms are agreed per order, in writing.
  • Where a partner installs or delivers, that partner's role and responsibility are named before work begins.

Who it is for

Buyers, builders and partners.

Young professionals equipping themselves for serious work. Small businesses that need reliable power more than they need a bigger generator. Schools, studios and organisations buying equipment in small volumes. Suppliers and installers who want a reliable route to customers. Anyone who wants to help put technology in the hands of a teenager who would otherwise wait years.
